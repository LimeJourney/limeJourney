LimeJourney

Every era rations something. Ours rationed the ability to build.

For half a century, software was expensive the way cathedrals were expensive: measured in years, headcount, and the patience of people willing to wait. In that time, the world made peace with mediocre tools, and ambition learned to queue.

Whole categories of ideas were never attempted, not particularly because they were wrong, but because they were priced out.

That constraint is dissolving in front of our eyes.

Intelligence is becoming infrastructure, as unremarkable and as consequential as the internet. When cognition itself can be provisioned, the cost of software collapses into the cost of judgment: knowing what deserves to exist, and having the nerve to build it.

We believe the next decade belongs to small groups with disproportionate conviction. Not the largest teams. The densest ones.

LimeJourney is a wager on that future. We build our own instruments for sales, for customer engagement, for support. And we build beside companies unwilling to wait for the old timelines.

Weeks where there were quarters. Individuals where there were departments.

Adapt or become the wreckage.
